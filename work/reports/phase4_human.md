## 5.1 (F0133)
- current: provide three months’ pay for the army, and add ten thousand measures of grain.
- problem: Greek: πεντήκοντα τάλαντα ... αὐτῷ δοῦσι παραχρῆμα τριμήνου μισθοδοτῆσαι τὴν δύναμιν καὶ σίτου προσθεῖναι μυριάδας. The fifty talents are the means of paying the army three months (δοῦσι ... μισθοδοτῆσαι), not a separate item; and μυριάδας stands with no numeral (a number has dropped out or been lost), so 'ten thousand' is supplied, not read.
- proposed: and with this provide three months’ pay for the army, and add tens of thousands of measures of grain.
- reason: δοῦσι…μισθοδοτῆσαι: the fifty talents are the three months’ pay. μυριάδας has no numeral; ‘ten thousand’ is supplied. Editor must decide on a numeral.

## 6.11 (F0009)
- current: [...] and for thirty years after that period, was continually being refined
- problem: Greek: '**** καὶ τριάκοντα ἔτεσιν ὕστερον ἀπὸ τούτων τῶν καιρῶν ἀεὶ ... προδιευκρινουμένων ἦν' = '[...] and thirty years later; from those times on it was continually being refined'. ἔτεσιν ὕστερον means 'years later' (a point in time), not 'for thirty years' (a duration), and ἀπὸ τούτων τῶν καιρῶν begins the refinement. 'καὶ τριάκοντα' may be the tail of a compound numeral lost in the lacuna, so 'thirty' may not be the whole figure.
- proposed: [...] and thirty years later; from that time on it was continually being refined
- reason: ἔτεσιν ὕστερον is a point in time, not a duration; lacuna may hide part of the numeral. Corrupt text, translator should confirm.
