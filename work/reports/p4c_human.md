# Phase 4c — items needing the translator/editor (NOT applied)

## 4.5 (F0118)
- current: he had in effect handed over the whole of his command to Dorimachus
- problem: Greek: παρακεχωρήκει τούτῳ τῆς ὅλης ἀρχῆς, after 'Δωριμάχου καὶ Σκόπα'. τούτῳ is singular and its antecedent is ambiguous; the nearest name is Scopas, and Scopas is the one who leads the action that follows. The English silently resolves it to Dorimachus. The singular may be a corruption of τούτοις (both).
- proposed: he had in effect handed over the whole of his command to the latter
- reason: Singular τούτῳ after 'Dorimachus and Scopas': likely Scopas (the nearest name, who then acts), but ambiguous; English silently chose Dorimachus.

## 33.4 (F0402)
- current: judging from appearances alone
- problem: Greek ἐκ πάντων is 'from all (indications)'; nothing in the Greek says 'alone'. The restriction to appearances alone is an addition.
- proposed: judging from every indication
- reason: 'alone' is added. ἐκ πάντων could mean 'from every indication' or 'out of all men'; the translator should choose.
