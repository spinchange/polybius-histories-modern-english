Greek: ἀλλὰ καθ᾽ ὃν καιρὸν ἐδίδοσαν τὴν ἐπιτροπήν, meaning they should have deliberated back when they surrendered, not now.

## Polybius_Histories_Book_36_modern_English.md, 36.15

## Polybius_Histories_Book_39_modern_English.md, 39.6
Greek: μέγαν καιρὸν ἐν τοῖς Ἕλλησιν ἔχων καὶ μεγάλην ἐξουσίαν. καιρός here means opportunity; "for a long time" has no basis in the Greek.
- OLD: although he had for a long time held great authority among the Greeks.
- NEW: although he had great opportunity and great power among the Greeks.

## Polybius_Histories_Book_39_modern_English.md, 39.7
Greek: Πτολεμαῖος ὁ τῆς Συρίας βασιλεύς. The chapter supports Philometor: the Alexandrians, being driven out by his brother, the brother's plot against Cyprus, and Lapethus. Philometor died of wounds in Syria in 145 BC, after he had taken the Syrian crown.
- OLD: When the king of Syria, Ptolemy, was struck in the course of the war, he died;
- NEW: When Ptolemy, king of Egypt, who had just become king of Syria as well, was struck in the course of the war, he died;
Consistency: the only other Ptolemy in Book 39 is Philopator (39.8), a different king. No other change needed.
