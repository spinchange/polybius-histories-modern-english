# Book 17 Note

## Greek Witness Note

νιηιλ ηυιυς λιβρι συπερεστ.

## English Reference Note (Perseus / 1889)

According to Hultsch no fragments or extracts of book 17 are preserved.
In it would have been contained the campaign of B. C. 199, in the war between
Rome and Philip, for which see Livy, 31, 34-43. And the operations of
Flamininus in the season of B. C. 198, Livy, 32, 9-18. The first seventeen
chapters of this book are generally classed in book 17.

## Modern English Translation

Nothing of this book survives.
