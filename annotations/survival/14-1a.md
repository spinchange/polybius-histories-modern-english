**Note:** The proem survives in the Vatican palimpsest of the Constantinian *De sententiis* (Vaticanus graecus 73), whose excerptor notes in a marginal scholion that only the proem of Book 14 could be read and that the leaves after it were lost; Dindorf's preface records the note at folio 29v. This rests on Didot's bracketed Greek and the scholion text in his edition, read with Dindorf's preface.
