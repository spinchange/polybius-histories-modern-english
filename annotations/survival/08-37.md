**Note:** This chapter has two sources. Paragraph 1 and paragraphs 12 to 13 are quoted by Suidas, the traditional name of the author of the Suda, a Byzantine lexicon of about 970. Paragraphs 2 to 11 survive in a Byzantine anthology of military excerpts (the Wescher codex, folio 100v), which is neither a Constantinian collection nor a quoting author. That is why the italic lines in the text mark only the Suda paragraphs.
