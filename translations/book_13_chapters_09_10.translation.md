# Book 13 Chapter 9: Antiochus in Arabia, B.C. 205-204

II. Asian Affairs

Labae, like Sabae, is a city of Chattenia, as Polybius says in his thirteenth book. The ethnic is Labaean, just as from Sabae comes Sabaean. Both belong to the same region, for Chattenia is territory of the Gerrhaeans.

Chattenia is the third district of the Gerrhaeans.

Polybius, Book 13: “Chattenia is otherwise a bleak land, but because of the prosperity of the Gerrhaeans it has been furnished with villages and towers; for they are the ones who occupy it.”

It lies on the Erythraean Sea. The inhabitants are called Chattenians, as he himself says: “For to these men he gave orders to spare the land of the Chattenians.”

The Gerrhaeans asked the king not to destroy what the gods had given them—that is, perpetual peace and freedom. When their letter had been interpreted to him, he said that he granted what they requested.

When freedom had been confirmed for the Gerrhaeans, they immediately honored King Antiochus with gifts of five hundred talents of silver, a thousand of frankincense, and two hundred of the substance called stacte. He then sailed on to the island of Tylos and from there departed for Seleuceia. These aromatic products came from the Erythraean Sea.

III. Miscellaneous Geographical Fragments

A. Italian Affairs

Badiza, a city of Bruttium, mentioned by Polybius in his thirteenth book.

Lampeteia, a city of Bruttium, mentioned by Polybius in his thirteenth book.

There is also Temese, both a city of Italy and a river. But Polybius, in the thirteenth book, calls the city Temeseia.

B. Greek and Macedonian Affairs

Allaria, a city of Crete, mentioned by Polybius in the thirteenth book. The ethnic is Allariates, as he himself says.

Ilattia, a city of Crete, mentioned by Polybius in his thirteenth book.

Sibyrtus, a city of Crete. The ethnic is Sibyrtius, as Polybius says in the thirteenth book.

Adrane, a city of Thrace ... but Polybius, in the thirteenth book, spells the middle vowel with eta, writing Adrēnē.

The Areian Plain ... there is also in Thrace a deserted plain with low-growing trees, as Polybius says in the thirteenth book.

Digeroi, a Thracian people, mentioned by Polybius in Book 13.

Cabyle, a city of Thrace not far from the territory of the Astae, mentioned by Polybius in the thirteenth book.

C. Fragment of Uncertain Location

Melitoussa, a city of Illyria, mentioned by Polybius in the thirteenth book.
